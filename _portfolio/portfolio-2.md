During my studies at Technische Universität Berlin I participated in the student project *[Research and Innovation in Student Exoskeleton development (RISE)](https://blogs.tu-berlin.de/mt_rise/de/home-deutsch/)*. RISE achieved the fourth place at the CYBATHLON 2024. I was a member of the working group for simulation and trajectory development. We used ROS2 to implement motion trajectories for various movements. Below you can see a visualization with RViz and images of the simulated exoskeleton in Gazebo.
